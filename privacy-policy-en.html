Privacy Policy — Yaomi

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Summary

Yaomi stores your calendar, to-do, and budget data on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The app contains no advertising and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

  • Calendar entries, event types, employee names and categories, to-do lists and tasks, budget months and transactions, and photos you attach.
  • This data stays in the app's private storage and is protected by iOS file protection.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. Use Export to keep a copy.

Wallet screenshots

  • When you import Wallet screenshots, the text is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework.
  • Screenshots are not uploaded anywhere. They are kept in memory only while you review them, and are not saved after the import.

Network requests

The app contacts these services only when you use the related feature:

  • date.nager.at: public holidays (country and region names and dates).
  • openholidaysapi.org: school holidays, when you download them (Yaomi Pro).
  • Calendar links you add yourself (iCal / webcal), refreshed every 6 hours (Yaomi Pro).
  • Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze, only when you tap their button.

These services receive only the request needed for the data, for example a country code and a year. They are operated by their own providers, under their own policies.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Calendars

When you allow access, the app reads and writes events in calendars on your device, such as iCloud or Google accounts that you have added to iOS. This data stays between your device and those calendars.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
